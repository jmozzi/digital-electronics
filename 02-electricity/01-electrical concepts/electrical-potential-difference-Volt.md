# Electrical Potential Difference (`V`): push
- the **push** that drives electrons to move (provided by a battery or power source)
- measured in `Volts (V)`
- electrons move from the negative side toward the positive side
- the energy becomes heat if not used properly by electrical component (e.g. light)
- connecting a wire directly from negative to positive (no component in the middle) creates a **short circuit**, allowing a very large current to flow with almost no resistance, creating a lot of heat in the wire and battery, which can lead to fire and/or damage
> Volt is kind of like the **water pressure**: higher voltage = stronger push
