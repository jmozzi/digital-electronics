# Electrical Power (`W`) - energy per second
- amount of power delivered over time (energy per second)
- measured in `Watts (W)`
- proportional to *flow of electrons (current) and charge imbalance (voltage)* that is pushing them through
	- aka proportional to `current` x `voltage`
	- `Power (W) = I x V`
- In a resistor, electrical power is converted into heat.
> Power is **total energy transferred per second**, '*how much work it can do per second*'.
> Or: power is the rate at which energy is used or transferred.
> **Energy**: total output, see [[energy-E]]
> **Power**: output per second
