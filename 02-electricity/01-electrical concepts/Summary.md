- **Current (I)** -> flow of charge (electrons moving)
- **Voltage (V)** -> the push that drives that flow
- **Power (P)** -> energy transferred per second 
- **Energy (E)** -> total effect produced over time 
- **Resistance (R)** -> controls how much current flows given a voltage (Ohm's law), see 

Energy is determined by **how much current the voltage can push**, which depends on resistance:
- P = I × V 
- V = I × R
- E = P × t
- Ohm’s law: I = V / R -> higher resistance -> less current -> less power -> less energy transferred per second, see [Ohms-law](../02-electrical-laws/Ohms-law.md), [Kirchhoff's-voltage-law-KVL](../02-electrical-laws/Kirchhoff's-voltage-law-KVL.md), [Kirchhoff's-current-law-KCL](../02-electrical-laws/Kirchhoff's-current-law-KCL.md)

|Law|Purpose|What it calculates|
|---|---|---|
|Ohm’s Law|Simple resistor|Voltage, current, resistance|
|Kirchhoff’s Laws|Complex circuits|Currents & voltages at junctions/loops|
|Power formula|Any circuit|Energy per second: P = V × I|
