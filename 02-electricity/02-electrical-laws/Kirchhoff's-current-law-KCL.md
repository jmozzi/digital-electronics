# Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL)*: 
**the sum of currents entering a node equals the sum of currents leaving the node.**
- Helps analyze **parallel circuits**
- Predict how current divides between branches

- series circuit:
	- current remains the same through both resistors in series
	- while voltage is split in proportion to the resistance (Ohm's law)
	- resistance in series is added together
- parallel circuit:
	- voltage remains constant across both parallel circuits (K's voltage law)
	- while current is split in proportion to the resistance (current has more than one path to flow)
	- adding two voltage sources in parallel does not increase voltage - it splits the current demand on each voltage source... *extends the life of a battery as each of them has a lower current draw.*
