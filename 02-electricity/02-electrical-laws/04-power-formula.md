## Calculate Power (W): 
-> Or: how power dissipates aka converts into heat across a resistor
- `P = I * V `
- `P = I^2 * R` 
- or if you know voltage across resistor and its resistance: `P = V^2 / R`  (by using Ohm's law and substituting)
- depending on what values are known
- usage: Check that resistors, traces, wires, and components **can handle the heat**
