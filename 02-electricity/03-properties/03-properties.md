| **Property**    | **Symbol** | **Unit**        | **Field Type** | **Opposes Change in...** |
| --------------- | ---------- | --------------- | -------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Resistance**  | $R$        | Ohms ($\Omega$) | None (Heat)    | Current Flow (constant)  |
| **Capacitance** | $C$        | Farads ($F$)    | Electrical     | **Voltage**              |
| **Inductance**  | $L$        | Henrys ($H$)    | Magnetic       | **Current**              |
# resistance
- impedes current flow
- hence dissipates energy
- the electrical energy is transformed into heat
- primary consumer of power
# capacitance
- stores energy in an *electrical* field
- *voltage* across a capacitance cannot change instantaneously
- cause it takes time to store energy, so it impedes changes in voltage
# inductance
- stores energy in a *magnetic* field
- *current* through an inductance cannot change instantaneously
- cause it takes time to store energy, so it impedes changes in current
# impedance
- all of the above
- **Resistance** is the opposition to current in **DC** (steady state). **Impedance** is the total opposition to current in **AC** (changing state).
> **The Formula Concept:** Impedance (Z) combines Resistance (R) and **Reactance** (X). Reactance is the "resistance" provided specifically by capacitors and inductors because of the frequency of the signal.
