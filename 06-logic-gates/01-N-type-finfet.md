# transistor - N type FinFet
- `1 V` applied -> electricity flows
- `0 V` applied -> no electricity flows
 
 Inside the transistor is a 
- `Gate`
- `Channel`
- And the `dielectric` (the barrier that separates the two and prevents electricity from passing through)
- On either side of the Gate and above the Gate are *metal contacts* connected to vertical `vias` that are used to 
	- input and output electricity to the corresponding parts.
- It is called `FinFet` due to its fin-like shape.

# N-type behaviour
